Privacy Policy

What this site and the Randall family hub collect, and what happens to it.

The public site

You can read loganbrandall.com without an account. The site has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. If you switch to dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it; it never leaves your device.

Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages see basic request information such as your IP address and browser. The site is hosted by Vercel, and fonts and a few scripts load from Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare), each under its own privacy policy.

The family hub

The family hub at /family is a private area for the Randall family. Only family members can sign in.

  • Signing in with Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the hub. They're used only to check that you're on the family list and to show who's signed in. The hub never sees your Google password and has no access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Signing in with the family password stores the name you type, so we know who added or edited a recipe.
  • A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to 60 days. It holds only your name, email (for Google sign-in) and an expiry date, and it's cleared when you sign out.
  • Recipes, stories and photos you add are stored privately with Vercel and can only be seen by people signed in to the hub.
  • Filling in a recipe from a link fetches that page (or reads the recipe text you paste) and sends it to Anthropic's Claude to pick out the recipe. Only the page or pasted text is sent, not who you are.
  • The family calendar shows events from the family's Google Calendar. The hub only reads it; it doesn't store or change any events.

Public recipes

A family member can make a recipe public, which gives it its own link to send to friends. Anyone with the link can see the recipe, its story and its photos; who added it is not shown. Public recipes aren't listed anywhere on this site and ask search engines not to index them. Making a recipe private again turns its link off right away.

What we don't do

Your information is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone outside the family, other than the services named above that keep the site and the hub running.

Removing your information

To have your information or anything you added removed, email logan.b.randall@gmail.com.
