About

About me.

I'm Logan Randall. I'm currently attending BYU-Idaho and pursuing an MBA, and I try to live by a simple idea: build, sustain, and grow. That goes for my career, my projects, and most of all, my family.

From Michigan to Idaho

I grew up in Michigan and moved to Idaho Falls during high school. Changing states partway through high school meant starting fresh, and it taught me how to adapt and make a new place feel like home.

Two years in Denver

I served a two-year church mission in Denver, Colorado. Those two years taught me how to work hard, talk with just about anyone, and put other people first, and I still lean on those lessons every day.

Family first

I met my beautiful wife, Marisa, while working at a local hot springs. My family is my motivation for everything I do. They're the reason I work hard in school, at work, and on everything I build.
